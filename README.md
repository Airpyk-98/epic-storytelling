A master viral storytelling and scriptwriting skill modeled on the high-retention, psychology-based storytelling style of **PsychToonsHQ**.

## Key Capabilities
- **Anti-Staccato Cadence**: Eliminates artificial, robotic conjunction splits (no chopping at `and`, `or`, `but`).
- **Plot-Driven Narrative Arcs**: Every psychological sign or concept is dramatized as a relatable micro-story with character stakes.
- **Continuous 60-Second Re-Hooks**: Sustains attention through curiosity loops, contrast, and perspective flips.
- **Street-Level Psychological Authority**: Deconstructs complex neurobiology into 5th-grade clear, relatable metaphors.
- **Observational Humor & Emotional Catharsis**: Balances deep emotional validation with self-aware, dry wit.
